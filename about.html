Our Story

Rooted in Anne Arundel County

Greenscape Landscaping started with nothing more than a single lawnmower, a reliable truck, and a drive to build beautiful outdoor spaces while studying at Anne Arundel Community College. What began as a way to pay for classes quickly grew through word-of-mouth, powered by an obsession with quality, hard work, and showing up on time.

Today, we have grown from that lone mower into a dedicated, professional crew. We proudly provide premium mowing, gardening, trimming, and custom landscape design for both homeowners and local businesses all across Anne Arundel County.
